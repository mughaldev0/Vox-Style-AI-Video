## ✨ What is this?

A tiny kit with everything you need to make Vox-style explainer videos with AI:

🗞️ **Paper-cutout collage** · 📰 **Halftone archival photos** · ✏️ **Hand-drawn annotation arrows**

Paste one master prompt into Claude, GPT or Gemini, give it a topic, and get ready-to-use video prompts for the tool you choose.

---

## 📂 What's inside

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| 📝 [**prompt.md**](prompt.md) | The master prompt. Paste it into Claude, GPT or Gemini. It asks for your topic and your video tool (Claude Opus, Gemini Veo 3 or Muse AI), then writes the video prompt. |
| 🍳 [**recipe.md**](recipe.md) | The exact workflow I used: topic → Claude → 3 prompts → Veo 3 → 3 clips → edit → final 30-second video. Includes fully styled 10-second prompt templates. |

## 🚀 Quick start

1. 📝 Open [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) and copy the master prompt.
2. 🤖 Paste it into Claude, GPT or Gemini.
3. 💬 Answer two questions: your **topic** and your **video tool**.
4. 📋 Copy the prompts it gives you into your video tool.
5. ✂️ Join the clips in an editing app (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere).

Want the full step-by-step? Read the [**recipe**](recipe.md).

## 🎨 The style

| Element | Look |
| --- | --- |
| 🗞️ Collage | Layered paper cutouts with torn edges and soft shadows |
| 📰 Photos | Black-and-white halftone archival images, one accent tint |
| ✏️ Annotations | Hand-drawn arrows, circles and handwritten labels |
| 🎞️ Motion | Stop-motion feel at about 12 fps |
| 🎨 Palette | Cream `#F5EFE0` · Ink `#1A1A1A` · Yellow `#FFD100` · Coral `#FF4B3E` · Teal `#2B8A8A` |
